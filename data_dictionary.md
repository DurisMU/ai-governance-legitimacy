# Data Dictionary — `cleaned_data.csv`

## Dataset overview

This dataset contains cleaned survey responses from a 2 (decision stakes) x 3
(explanation type) between-subjects experiment on how algorithmic explanations
shape citizens' perceptions of institutional legitimacy in AI-enabled governance.

- **Sample size:** N = 420 (70 participants per cell)
- **Design:** 2 x 3 fully-crossed, balanced between-subjects design
- **Levels of Factor A (Decision Stakes):** Low Stakes (parking fine), High Stakes (welfare-assistance denial)
- **Levels of Factor B (Explanation Type):** Outcome, Process, Empathic
- **Response scale:** all Likert-style items are rated on a 5-point scale (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)

The file `anova_summary.csv` contains pre-computed 2x3 ANOVA summaries and
group descriptive statistics for convenience; `descriptive_stats.csv` and
`anova_results.csv` are regenerated by `code/analysis.py`.

## Construct definitions

- **Perceived Institutional Legitimacy (PIL)** — primary dependent variable: the
  extent to which participants judge the AI-assisted government decision as
  institutionally legitimate, reasonable, and authoritative.
- **Institutional Trust (IT)** — trust in the government agency making the decision.
- **Policy Acceptance (PA)** — willingness to accept and comply with the specific decision.
- **Government Trust (GT)** — general dispositional trust in government (control).
- **AI Attitude (AA)** — general attitude toward AI technology (control).
- **AI Experience (AE)** — prior experience with AI applications (control).
- **Policy Attention (PAC)** — attention to public-policy affairs (control).

## Notes

- Composite scores (`*_Mean`) are the unweighted mean of their items.
- There is no `Participant_ID` column; rows are anonymous and order-preserved.
- Raw (pre-cleaning) survey exports are not included in this repository; this
  cleaned file is the analytical starting point.
